Group trips, from plan to memory.

Trippy is an app for your group to plan the trip together and keep what you lived at every place.

Join the waitlist See how it works

Everyone suggests, one itinerary.

Everyone adds the places they want to see, by name, by address or by pasting a Google Maps link. The organizer builds the itinerary, day by day.

The plan comes along on the trip.

The group sees what is on today and the next place, and logs every visit with photos, notes and who went.

Everyone’s photos, in one place.

Everyone’s memories stay together, place by place. At the end they become a trip recap: an album with highlights and a short video.
